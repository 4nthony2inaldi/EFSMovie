Bidding System
  • Minimum bid: $1 per movie
  • Win up to 2 movies per auction
  • Highest bid wins the movie
  • Set priorities if you might win 3+
  • Bids are blind — you can't see others
Pro Tip: Use "$1 on All" to quickly bid minimum on everything!

Tie-Breaking Rules

When two teams bid the same amount:

  • Lower-ranked team wins
    Helps underdogs catch up!
  • Same rank? Random selection
Strategy: Being behind in standings gives you tie-break advantage!

Scoring Formula
How Points Are Calculated
Final Score = Base Score + Oscar Points
Base Score = min(15, (Domestic BO ÷ Theaters) ÷ 1000) × Metacritic%
💵 Box Office
  • Domestic gross ÷ theater count
  • Capped at 15 max
  • Must be in 5+ theaters
📊 Metacritic
  • Score from 0-100
  • Used as multiplier
  • 85% = ×0.85
🏆 Oscars
  • Nomination: +0.5 pts
  • Win: +1.0 pts
  • Best Picture: 2× bonus
🎬 Blockbuster Hit
  • $100M domestic ÷ 4,000 theaters = 25 → capped at 15
  • 15 × 0.72 Metacritic = 10.8
Final: 10.8 pts
🎭 Oscar Darling
  • $15M ÷ 1,500 theaters = 10 × 0.92 = 9.2
  • + 6 noms (3pts) + 2 wins (2pts) + BP nom (0.5pt)
Final: 14.7 pts

Winning Strategies
  • Diversify your roster
    Don't blow budget on one movie
  • Target Oscar bait
    Prestige films score big late in season
  • Watch for blockbusters
    High box office = solid base score
  • Find hidden gems
    High Metacritic + moderate BO
  • Save for late season
    Oscar contenders release late
Remember: You can add up to 2 movies per auction from TMDB if something is missing!
