Movie League Guide

Your complete guide to auctions, bidding, and scoring

🎬 How Auctions Work
  • Opens - Browse movies, place bids
  • Bidding - Modify bids anytime while open
  • Closes - No more changes allowed
  • Resolves - Winners assigned automatically
You can add up to 2 movies per auction from TMDB!
💰 Bidding System
  • Min bid: $1 - No maximum per movie
  • Win up to 2 movies per auction
  • Highest bid wins the movie
  • Set priorities if you might win 3+
Use "$1 on All" to quickly bid minimum on everything!
⭐ Scoring Formula
Box Office = min(15, (Domestic BO ÷ Theaters) ÷ 1000)
Base Score = Box Office × Metacritic (0-1)
Final = Base + Oscar Points
  • Oscar Nom: +0.5 pts each
  • Oscar Win: +1.0 pts each
  • Best Picture: +0.5 nom / +1.0 win bonus
💵 Budget Management
  • Start with $1,000 for the season
  • Budget deducted when you win
  • Cannot bid more than remaining budget
  • No bids? Auto-assigned 2 movies @ ~$20
Plan ahead - budget must last all auctions!
⚖️ Tie-Breakers

When two teams bid the same amount:

  • Lower-ranked team wins (helps underdogs!)
  • Same rank? Random selection
Being behind in standings gives you tie-break advantage!
💡 Winning Strategies
  • Diversify - Don't blow budget on one movie
  • Oscar bait - Prestige films score big late
  • Blockbusters - High box office = base score
  • Hidden gems - High Metacritic + moderate BO
📊 Score Examples

Blockbuster Hit

  • $100M domestic / 2,500 theaters = 40
  • Capped at 15, × 0.85 Metacritic
  • Final: 12.75 pts

Oscar Darling

  • $10M / 1,000 theaters × 0.75 = 7.5
  • + 5 noms, 2 wins, BP nom = +5 pts
